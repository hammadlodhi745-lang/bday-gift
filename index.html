Meri pyari Shajiha Jaan,

Is zindagi mein aapka hona aisi rehmat hai jiske laayiq main khud ko nahi samajhta, magar aapke saath guzra har lamha mere liye qeemti hai. Aapki muskurahat, aapka sukoon aur aapki narmi har cheez ko khoobsurat bana deti hai. Main in baaton ko kabhi khona nahi chahta.

Main dua karta hoon ke Allah aapko hamesha khush, mehfooz aur pur-sukoon rakhe, aur aapke gird pyaar rahe. Har chhote lamhe, har khamosh muskurahat aur hamari saari yaadon ke liye shukriya.

Meri dua hai ke hum hamesha ek doosre ko samajhte rahein, ek doosre ka saath chunte rahein aur pyaar, sabr aur sachchai ke saath khoobsurat yaadein banate rahein. Aap meri zindagi ke sab se pyare aur qeemti hisson mein se ek hain.
